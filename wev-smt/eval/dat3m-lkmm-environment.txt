<INSTANCE_ID> is HARD-n{n}-{hex(java String.hashCode of the expression)},
the identifier HardExprBenchRun gives the instance. Rows therefore join to
eval/generated-hard-pairs.csv on (expr, threads).

linux-kernel.cat with --target=lkmm is used because LKMM is the deployed
model whose ordering is syntactic-dependency based; IMM and RC11 reject
READ_ONCE/WRITE_ONCE programs outright. This is the same model and the same
invocation shape as the 41-test cross-check recorded in
eval/dat3m-command-lines.txt.
